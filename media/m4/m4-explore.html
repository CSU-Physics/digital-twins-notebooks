A straight line or physics, beyond the training range

Simulated runs of the course's furnace at 1000 °C in dry O₂ on bare wafers. Two models are fitted to the training runs: a data-driven straight line in oxidation time, and a hybrid: the textbook Deal-Grove curve plus a straight-line correction learned from its errors. Move the prediction time out of the training range and compare their errors with the truth, which only a simulation can show.
