Calibrate, then predict with two bands

Simulated calibration runs at 1000 °C in dry O₂ on wafers with a 25 nm starting oxide, measured with a known standard deviation σ. The explorer fits B and B/A by weighted least squares, as the lab's helper does, and draws the 95% confidence band for the predicted mean and the 95% prediction band for one new measurement. Here the simulated furnace follows the Deal-Grove model exactly, so only noise and the choice of runs matter.

Calibration design

The runs are spread evenly from 30 minutes to the longest run.
