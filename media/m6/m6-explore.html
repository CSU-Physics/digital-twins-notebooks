Load and tools

Each replication runs 600 hours from an empty line; the first 100 hours are the warm-up and are left out. Replications share their random numbers whatever the settings, so comparisons are paired. This page uses its own random numbers: its values differ a little from the lab's, and the shapes agree.

Calibrate on a calendar, or when the drift estimate says so

One furnace tube runs 170 times. Its thermocouple bias grows with every run; blue is the thickness drift it causes, maroon the Module 5 Kalman estimate of it. Beyond 5 nm the controller can no longer correct and lots are off target. Both rules see the same tube and noise; a calibration takes the tube out for 8 hours.

The tube and the rules

Simplified from the lab's line model: bias growth 0.06 to 0.09 °C per run, 0.52 nm per °C, a failing thermocouple adds 0.35 °C per run, a calibration resets the tube.
