When does the wafer's result arrive?

Two clocks, one hold mean

The furnace controller stamps its readings with its own clock; the MES writes the start and end of the hold with the fab's clock. The twin needs the mean zone temperature over the 60-minute hold. If the two clocks disagree and the twin picks readings by the MES times, it averages the wrong part of the run. Every value here is simulated.

Settings

Recipe: ramp up from 800 °C at about 6.7 °C per minute for 30 minutes, hold for 60 minutes, ramp down at 5 °C per minute for 40 minutes. One reading every 10 s.

The run, on the fab's clock

Leakage: choosing signals with the test records

Made-up records with random signals. The earliest 70% train, the latest 30% test. In each repetition the top signals are ranked by S2N two ways, on the training records only and on all the records, and the same simple classifier is fitted on the training records and scored on the test records. With no real signal, the right answer is an AUC of 0.5.
